# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not post other people's device data, logs, or private
  files.
- Do not request or share proprietary APKs or recovered third-party code.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and evidence quality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, publishing private
information, or encouraging unauthorized analysis of third-party applications.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the repository maintainers' GitHub contact
or a security advisory if confidentiality is required. Maintainers may remove
content or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
